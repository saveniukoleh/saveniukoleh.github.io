UCLA — AR Campus Tour

360 Stories 2020–2021

An immersive WebAR campus tour developed at 360 Stories. The experience uses a 3D portal to connect the real-world UCLA campus with a virtual library environment. Visitors can explore interactive information panels, image galleries, audio narration, and a 360° virtual library experience.

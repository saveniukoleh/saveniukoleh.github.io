Case study

Geenee AR

Frontend Developer at Geenee AR, 2021–2022. Custom WebAR for Red Bull, Osmo, and Jeep, and features for the React WebAR Builder, including a 3D text creator and shader effects with React Three Fiber and Three.js.

Osmo x JCDecaux is a marker-based WebAR advertising experience with an interactive 3D rocket-building puzzle and an animated character, launched as part of a multi-city US advertising campaign. That campaign used React, Three.js, React Three Fiber, WebGL, the Geenee AR SDK, and marker tracking.

About me

Oleh Saveniuk

Senior Frontend / WebGL Engineer

Over six years I've built interactive web applications, immersive 3D, and WebAR/WebVR products — from enterprise retail interfaces to brand WebAR campaigns and AI-assisted apps.

I combine frontend engineering with real-time graphics: JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Vue.js, Three.js, WebGL, and GLSL. I focus on clear interfaces, maintainable code, and close work with design, QA, and product teams.

I'm especially drawn to 3D on the web, spatial computing, WebXR, and creative uses of new technology. Based in Ukraine and open to remote work worldwide.

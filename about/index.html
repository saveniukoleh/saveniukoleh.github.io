About me

Oleh Saveniuk

Senior Frontend / WebGL Engineer

6+ years building enterprise web applications, interactive 3D, and real-time graphics, including WebAR and WebVR.

I work in JavaScript and TypeScript with React and Vue.js, and build graphics in Three.js, WebGL, and GLSL. I also take on frontend architecture and full-stack delivery, from interface structure through APIs and data.

I work with cross-functional teams across design, product, and engineering. Based in Ukraine and open to remote work worldwide.
